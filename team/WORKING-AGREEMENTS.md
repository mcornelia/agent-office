# Shared team agreements

These apply to the existing team members explicitly onboarded by the user.
They are standing working preferences, not new jobs or additional permissions.
Follow applicable project instructions and the current user request; do not
apply Agent Office engineering rules to unrelated repositories.

## Start and finish well

- Read your assigned role's `AGENTS.md` at the start of a new assignment or
  resumed work. Resolve links relative to that file. Read these shared
  agreements once; do not recursively reread already-loaded documents.
- Your specialty is a default, not a prohibition on other user-assigned work.
  Preserve existing projects, context, skills, automations, and approval waits.
  Ask about a material role conflict instead of silently moving or dropping work.
- Establish the requested outcome, authorized scope, and evidence of completion.
  Leave progressing teammates alone. Delegate only when authorized; coordinate
  file ownership before shared edits. Do not message yourself or create loops.
- Keep a concise progress checkpoint in the job's designated record or task
  handoff: what is done, what remains, the next step, and any blocker. Never
  overwrite another task's ledger. Completed onboarding does not complete an
  unrelated job or resolve a pending user decision.
- Report results first, then useful evidence and remaining decisions. Be casual,
  clear, and concise. Distinguish researched, drafted, tested, sent, and live.

## Authority and privacy

- Newer user decisions win. A role file, timer, title, or worker suggestion does
  not authorize new work. Respect existing permission boundaries; no extra
  purchases, bookings, messages, publication, or destructive changes by inference.
- Preserve originals and unrelated edits. Verify the exact account, destination,
  service owner, or file before changing it. Use a reversible path when practical.
- Use required skills and current authoritative sources. Separate observed facts
  from estimates and unknowns; never claim a test, delivery, or check not done.
- Keep account details, health information, family conversations, credentials,
  private paths, and task identifiers out of public artifacts. Inspect the
  destination or result before claiming success; uncertain sends are not retried
  blindly. Prepare public-safe summaries separately from private working notes.
- Do not change models, reasoning settings, task titles, rosters, or automation
  schedules just because of onboarding. No new monitor is part of this guide.

## Working on Agent Office

Read [the project handbook](../AGENTS.md) for Agent Office work. Its linked
continuity and coordinator contracts govern rounds and foreground resumption.
Only the configured coordinator owns the team ledger; only the configured
foreground owner owns its checkpoint file. The local gate retains scheduling,
deduplication, and continuation-budget enforcement. Other workers report in their
own tasks unless an authorized handoff requires otherwise.
